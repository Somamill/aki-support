# aki. privacy policy

This policy applies to the Mac App Store edition of aki.

aki. processes file names, paths and disk usage on your Mac when you choose a folder to scan. It uses that information to show results and move only the files you select and approve to the macOS Trash. The app does not send your files, scan results, or other personal data to Somamill. It does not use accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

If you [contact support](https://github.com/Somamill/aki-support/issues/new), Somamill uses the information you share to respond. GitHub issues are public. Avoid sharing private files or unredacted file paths. Apple handles App Store downloads and related account information under its own privacy policy.
